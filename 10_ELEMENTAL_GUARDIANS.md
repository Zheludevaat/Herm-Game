# 10_ELEMENTAL_GUARDIANS

Status: Canonical  
Authority level: Tier 1  
Primary scope: Elemental Guardian reception at death

## The four Guardians
- Zephyros | Air | clarity
- Pyralis | Fire | warmth
- Undine | Water | comfort
- Chthonia | Earth | grounding

## Fixed order
The order never changes:
1. clarity
2. warmth
3. comfort
4. grounding

## Function
- facilitate bodily dissolution
- orient the soul to discarnate existence
- receive, not judge

## Relation to residue
- they loosen elemental residue
- they do not force release of residue
- resistance shapes later sublunary vulnerability

## Duration
Reception may take moments or decades in subjective time.

## Handoff
- after their work is complete, the personal daimon becomes directly perceptible
- Guardians do not travel onward through the whole journey

## Distinctions
They are not:
- Governors
- personal daimons
- sphere-daimons
- tribunal members

## Compact Retrieval Block
- 4 Guardians
- fixed order always
- receive, do not judge
- facilitate body dissolution
- loosen residue
- then daimon becomes perceptible
