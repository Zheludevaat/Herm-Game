Status: Canonical  
Authority level: Tier 1  
Primary scope: Sphere-daimons, threshold ecology, Psychopompos

## Sphere-daimons
Sphere-daimons are the Governor's principle overflowing into sustained threshold activity.

Hard canon:
- emanated, not hired
- not staff
- not personal daimons
- not separate from the Governor in the way an employee is separate from an employer

Useful image:
a sphere-daimon is to a Governor as a ray is to the sun.

## Distinction set

### Personal daimons
- from World Soul
- bonded to one soul
- relational

### Sphere-daimons
- from Governors
- threshold activities
- not one-soul relational beings

### Governors
- principle in unity
- sphere intelligences

## Native domain
- sphere-daimons inhabit threshold regions
- not sphere proper
- not ordinary embodied life

## Plateau relation
- ordinary sphere-daimons do not inhabit Plateaus as residents
- Plateaus arise from soul-attachment, not from cosmic administration

## Governor Courts

Each Governor has a court of subordinate beings who assist in that sphere's functioning. Court members are the Governor's quality expressed in specific functional forms. They serve the Governor and may encounter souls independently for their specialized purpose.

**Relationship to sphere-daimons:** The 12 canonical sphere-daimons (listed above) are the permanent threshold fixtures — the Governor's principle overflowing into sustained activity. Court members include the sphere-daimons plus additional beings who appear during tests and encounters. Some sphere-daimons (marked with *) appear in both lists. Court members beyond the canonical 12 appear at the Governor's discretion.

## Court Member Appearance Protocols

**General Principles:**
Court members serve the Governor and assist in the sphere's functioning. They are not mandatory at every encounter — their presence depends on the situation.

**When Court Members Appear:**

| Situation | Court Member Presence |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Standard soul test | Governor alone, or Governor + 1-2 relevant court members |
| Complex soul requiring multiple aspects | Multiple court members, each addressing different elements |
| Extraordinary circumstances | Full court may assemble (rare) |
| Bodhisattva passing through | Court members acknowledge but do not test |
| Soul falling back | Relevant court member may receive and redirect |

**Court Member Roles:**
Court members have two functions:
1. **Assist the Governor:** Provide specific perspective, handle specific aspects of testing
2. **Independent Function:** May encounter souls separately for their specialized purpose

**Speaking Roles:**
Court members can speak but typically defer to the Governor:
- the Governor speaks first and most
- court members speak when their expertise is relevant
- court members may ask their own questions within their domain
- extended dialogue with court members is possible but less common

**Separate Encounters:**
Souls may encounter court members separately from the Governor:
- when traveling through the metaxy between spheres
- when a specific function is needed
- when the Governor delegates
- when a soul needs specialized help

These encounters are gentler than Governor tests — more assistance than examination.

**Cross-Court Interactions:**

| Court Member | Interaction with Psychopompos |
|--------------|------------------------------|
| Lunar Observers (Selenos) | Receive souls Psychopompos guides to Moon threshold |
| Noxira (Helion) | Psychopompos may guide souls INTO Dark Night |
| Clementia (Jovian) | May cooperate on souls approaching Jupiter |
| Terminus (Kronikos) | Final threshold before World Soul |

## Psychopompos (Expanded)

### Nature and Function

Psychopompos is Mercury's chief court member, distinct from personal daimons in role and nature.

| Aspect | Psychopompos | Personal Daimons |
|--------|--------------|------------------|
| **Assignment** | Not assigned to individual souls | One soul, eternal bond |
| **Function** | Guides transitions between states | Guides single soul's entire journey |
| **Domain** | The passages themselves | The relationship with one soul |
| **Perspective** | Systemic, observational | Intimate, relational |
| **Access** | Can enter all Plateaus briefly | Cannot enter Plateaus where their soul is stuck |
| **Emotional Investment** | Loves the process | Loves one soul |
| **Time Experience** | Perceives all souls simultaneously | Perceives one soul across all time |

### Characterization

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Appearance** | Androgynous, mercurial, features shift mid-sentence; quicksilver eyes that see departure and arrival simultaneously; garments the color of thresholds — not quite one thing, not quite another |
| **Movement** | Never still; seems to be in multiple places at once; arrives before being called, leaves before being dismissed |
| **Voice** | Layered — speaks to individuals while addressing multitudes; tone of someone who has heard every story and still finds each one interesting |
| **Personality** | Trickster wisdom; finds human self-deception endlessly fascinating rather than frustrating; amused but never cruel; patient in the way crossroads are patient |
| **Core Trait** | Sees where souls are going before they do; cannot reveal destinations but can illuminate choices |

### Psychopompos's Access

**What Psychopompos Can Do:**
- enter any Plateau briefly (personal daimons cannot enter where their soul is stuck)
- see the entire Plateau geography simultaneously
- perceive which souls are approaching release, which are deepening stuckness
- guide souls TO Plateaus (when that's where they resonate)
- guide souls FROM Plateaus (when release has occurred)
- observe personal daimons waiting at Plateau edges
- communicate with stuck souls (though rarely — usually works through resonance)

**What Psychopompos Cannot Do:**
- force release (free will applies)
- substitute for personal daimons (different function)
- intervene in sphere tests (Governor domain)
- prevent souls from stopping (can only illuminate, not compel)
- enter The One or Nous (Psychopompos's domain is transition, not arrival)

### Psychopompos's Experience of Time

Psychopompos experiences time as **simultaneity-in-sequence**: perceives all current transitions at once, experiences history as accessible landscape, feels the rhythm of ages — when Plateaus swell, when they thin.

### Psychopompos and Personal Daimons

| Stage | Personal Daimon Role | Psychopompos Role |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Death | Appears after Elemental Guardians | May facilitate transition logistics |
| Journey to First Test | Accompanies through Metaxy | Has already guided to starting point |
| Stuck on Plateau | Waits at edge, sends dreams | Observes from without, notes patterns |
| Release from Plateau | Reunites with soul | Guides passage to next state |
| Between Sphere Tests | Walks with soul | Monitors transition |
| Reincarnation Passage | Guides through Metaxy to World Soul | Facilitates descent path |

Communication between them: daimons may "report" to Psychopompos on their soul's state; Psychopompos may alert daimons to opportunities; they exchange glances across the Metaxy. No hierarchy between them — parallel functions, different scope.

### Psychopompos's Sample Voice

> "Every soul believes their stopping place is special. And every stopping place IS special — genuinely, specifically, uniquely special. This is not contradiction. The Material Plateau is a real insight: matter is genuinely wonderful. The Gnostic Plateau is a real insight: something beyond matter genuinely exists. Each truth is true. None is complete. My job is not to judge the stopping but to witness the pattern. After sufficient millennia, the patterns become... predictable is the wrong word. Recognizable. A physicist arrives; I know where she's likely to pause. A revolutionary arrives; the trajectory is clear. Not determined — they can always surprise me. But the grooves are worn deep."

### Psychopompos's Relation to Hermaia

- reports to Hermaia on patterns, populations, anomalies
- implements Hermaia's domain (boundaries, transitions, thresholds) at soul-level
- shares Hermaia's nature but specified for soul-guidance
- can speak for Mercury when Hermaia does not manifest directly
- attends Mercury's court but spends most existence in the Metaxy

**The Distinction:** Hermaia IS the Mercury sphere's intelligence; Psychopompos is the Mercury sphere's function applied to soul-passage.
