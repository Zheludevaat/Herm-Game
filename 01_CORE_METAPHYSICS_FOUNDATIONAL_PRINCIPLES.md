# 01_CORE_METAPHYSICS_FOUNDATIONAL_PRINCIPLES

Status: Canonical  
Authority level: Tier 1  
Primary scope: Foundational metaphysical stance

## Purpose

Defines what kind of cosmos this is, what the anthology affirms, and what it rejects.

## Hard Canon

### Primary stance
- Hermetic Neoplatonism, not Gnosticism
- cosmos is ordered, purposeful, educative, beautiful, difficult, fundamentally good
- cosmos is a school, not a prison
- incarnation is not punishment
- matter is real and sacred
- body is a vehicle for learning, not a tomb

### Rejected stance
The anthology rejects as narrative truth:
- evil matter
- malicious archons
- punitive reincarnation
- cosmic courtroom metaphysics
- damned-soul logic
- cynical emptiness

Characters may believe these things. The narrative may not affirm them.

### Fate and freedom
- fate is real at garment level
- freedom is real through nous
- hierarchy resolves the tension
- fate governs what has not yet been transcended

### Suffering
- suffering is real
- suffering is not proof of cosmic malice
- suffering is part of the curriculum
- suffering does not justify contempt toward stuck souls

### Evil
- evil is privation or distance from source
- not a rival cosmic power
- not baked into matter as hostile essence

### No adversarial cosmic jailers
- no demons, archons, prison-wardens, or malicious cosmic captors
- obstacles are attachments and confusions
- Governors are testers, not villains

### Comedy and metaphysics
Comedy is possible because reality is meaningful. Souls mistake partial truths for total truths.

## What This Project Is
- cosmically affirmative
- serious about suffering
- metaphysically serious
- comic in mode
- hierarchical in ontology
- open on ultimate questions

## What This Project Is Not
- Gnostic prison fiction
- punitive karmic bureaucracy
- cynical satire
- ideological sorting machine
- contempt for embodiment

## Open Questions
Do not close casually:
- what happens after union
- whether all souls complete the journey
- full persistence of individuality in union
- final nature of evil
- exact positive description of The One

## Narrative Constraints
- do not frame embodiment as cosmic mistake
- do not imply suffering proves a malicious cosmos
- do not solve open questions with certainty
- do not mock embodied life from above

## Compact Retrieval Block
- Hermetic not Gnostic
- school not prison
- matter sacred
- incarnation opportunity
- suffering real but not proof of malice
- fate below, freedom through nous
- no adversarial jailers
- comedy from meaningful reality
- open questions remain open
