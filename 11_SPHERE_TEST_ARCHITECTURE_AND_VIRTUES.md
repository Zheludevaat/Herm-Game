# 11_SPHERE_TEST_ARCHITECTURE_AND_VIRTUES

Status: Canonical
Authority level: Tier 1
Primary scope: Seven sphere tests and virtues

## Core principle
The seven planetary spheres are seven modes of being encountered in ascent.

Hard canon:
- ascent order: Moon -> Mercury -> Venus -> Sun -> Mars -> Jupiter -> Saturn
- passing is release, not victory
- failing is unreadiness, not punishment

## Sphere tests

### Moon | Selenos | Phantasia | virtue: Clarity
Test: distinguish self from dream and endless becoming
Domain (Story 36): shows the soul what it believes that isn't true
Note: the Moon sphere may operate in a revelatory mode — showing the soul the true source (love,
pattern, genuine vocation) of a quality before that quality is released, rather than requiring
immediate release. The release of the misidentified version then occurs at the appropriate sphere.

### Mercury | Hermaia | Metis | virtue: Simplicity
Test: stop explaining and become present to what reason did not create
Domain (Story 36): shows the soul what it misunderstands about itself

### Venus | Kypria | Eros | virtue: Appreciation
Test: release grasping without rejecting love
Domain (Story 36): shows the soul what it loves that it doesn't admit to loving

### Sun | Helion | Eidōlon | virtue: Presence
Test: remain when self-image collapses
Domain (Story 36): shows the soul who it is when everything else is removed

### Mars | Areon | Thumos | virtue: Stillness
Test: put down the sword without ceasing to be
Domain (Story 36): shows the soul what it uses its will for

### Jupiter | Jovian | Philotimia | virtue: Openness
Test: release the need to complete, judge, or contain final order
Domain (Story 36): shows the soul what it thinks justice means

### Saturn | Kronikos | Anankē | virtue: Freedom
Test: release even the true but incomplete final framework
Domain (Story 36): shows the soul what it believes must be true that doesn't have to be

## Between tests
- soul rests in the Metaxy
- personal daimon present
- newly gained virtue must be integrated
- duration varies

## Cumulative transformation
Later tests are encountered by a soul already changed by earlier releases.

## Post-sphere fall
See 12_GOVERNORS_AND_TEST_FAILURE_MECHANICS for full treatment.
A soul that completes all seven tests may still fall back if the engine gamed the tests without
genuine release. The fall returns the soul to the Plateau of the engine's governing quality.
