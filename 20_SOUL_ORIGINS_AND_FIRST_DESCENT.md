- souls are not created in time
- unity in The One
- individuation through emanation, not fabrication

## Individuation sequence
1. The One: undifferentiated unity
2. Nous: intellectual individuation
3. World Soul: potential individuation
4. first descent: actual individuation

## First-timers

- no pattern yet
- no karmic gravity
- daimon assigned at first individuation into the journey
- first life configured by essence + cosmic moment

First-timers are not blank slates.

## Pre-incarnate World Soul
For first-timers:
- anticipation rather than homecoming
- fellow potential sensed
- returning souls feel denser due to pattern
- Pneumara appears in sending-forth mode

## Equality rule
First-timers and returners:
- same Governors
- same Plateaus
- same structure
- same opportunity for completion

- same daimon assigned at first individuation into journey
