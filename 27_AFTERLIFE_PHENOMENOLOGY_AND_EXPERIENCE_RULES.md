# 27_AFTERLIFE_PHENOMENOLOGY_AND_EXPERIENCE_RULES

Status: Canonical / Operational  
Authority level: Tier 1 for phenomenology, Tier 3 for scene-use  
Primary scope: What discarnate existence feels like

## Foundational principle
Phenomenology is not mechanics.
Use this file for scene-feel, not for resolving structural disputes.

## Discarnate baseline
After death:
- body gone
- garments remain
- identity, thought, imagination, desire, and pattern remain
- vivid life continues in altered medium

## Communication
Discarnate souls communicate through garments.

- Mercury: verbal capacity
- Moon: imagery
- Venus: emotional coloring
- Sun: self-framing

### By level
- sublunary: closest to ordinary speech
- Plateaus: garment-dominant communication
- active passage: quieter, cleaner, less performative
- after multiple releases: less verbal, more direct
- World Soul: essence-level affect, not garment emotion

## Emotional phenomenology
- emotions remain real after death
- Plateaus amplify the emotional register of the attachment sustaining them
- crack-events often arrive through emotions the Plateau cannot metabolize

## World Soul emotional paradox
At World Soul:
- garments gone
- garment-emotions gone
- what remains is essence-emotion
- closest descriptor is love without object-grasping

## Dreams on Plateaus

### Can souls dream on Plateaus?
Yes. Though sleep is not required, it remains possible. Many souls, especially those long-resident, fall into states resembling sleep. In these states, they dream.

### The nature of Plateau dreams

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Content | Often memories, symbols, or variations on the Plateau's theme |
| Vividness | More vivid than life-dreams — the afterlife imagination is stronger |
| Symbolism | Highly symbolic — the unconscious speaks in images |
| Memory | May or may not be remembered upon waking |

### Dreams as daimonic channel
The personal daimon CAN reach stuck souls through dreams:
- dreams are a liminal state — the soul's defenses are lower
- the barrier that prevents direct contact is thinner in dreams
- the daimon can plant images, questions, memories
- the soul does NOT know the dream comes from their daimon

What daimons can do through dreams: send symbolic messages (images that crack the framework), surface buried memories (life experiences that contradict current certainty), ask questions (questions that arise in the dream and persist after waking), create discomfort (nightmares that make the Plateau feel less certain), show alternatives (visions of what lies beyond).

What daimons cannot do through dreams: force the soul to follow the message, communicate directly ("I am your daimon, listen to me"), override free will, bypass the work the soul must do.

### Dream types on Plateaus

| Type | Description | Function |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Memory Dreams | Reliving life experiences | Surfaces forgotten truths |
| Symbolic Dreams | Abstract imagery with meaning | Communicates what can't be said directly |
| Prophetic Dreams | Glimpses of the journey ahead | Creates desire to move |
| Cracking Dreams | Images that don't fit the framework | Catalyzes questioning |
| Visitation Dreams | Encounter with another being | May be daimonic or genuine soul-meeting |
| Nightmare | Disturbing content | Can shake false certainty |

### Dreams at different levels

| Level | Dream Quality |
|-------|---------------|
| Sublunary Plateaus | Most material, memory-heavy |
| Moon Plateaus | Most vivid — imagination dominant |
| Mercury/Venus | Symbolic, relational |
| Sun | Revelatory, uncomfortable |
| Mars/Jupiter | Consequential, demanding |
| Saturn | Ancient, heavy with time |
| Nous | Dreams become contemplation — hard to distinguish |

## Scene-use rules
- full-garment souls still sound largely person-like
- high-level speech must become cleaner and stranger
- do not reduce all afterlife scenes to ghost-talk or mystical fog

## Compact Retrieval Block
- phenomenology = what it feels like
- body lost, garments retained after death
- communication is garment-mediated
- communication changes with release
- Plateaus amplify sustaining emotional weather
- World Soul emotion = essence-level love
- Plateau dreams: possible, vivid, symbolic, more intense than life-dreams
- dreams are primary daimonic channel on Plateaus (liminal state, thinner barriers)
- daimons can plant images/questions/memories in dreams but cannot force, communicate directly, or override free will
- 6 dream types: memory, symbolic, prophetic, cracking, visitation, nightmare
- dream quality varies by level: sublunary (material) → Moon (vivid) → Sun (revelatory) → Saturn (ancient) → Nous (indistinguishable from contemplation)
