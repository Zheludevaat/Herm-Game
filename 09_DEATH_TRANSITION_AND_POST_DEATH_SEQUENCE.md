# 09_DEATH_TRANSITION_AND_POST_DEATH_SEQUENCE

Status: Canonical  
Authority level: Tier 1  
Primary scope: Death, transition, sublunary afterlife sequence

## Core rule
Death circumstance changes transition, not curriculum.

- no manner of death prevents ascent
- no manner of death guarantees ascent
- cosmos does not compound suffering with punishment

## Standard sequence

1. bodily death  
2. Elemental Guardian reception  
3. awareness of discarnate state  
4. possible Psychopompos orientation  
5. daimon visibility  
6. sublunary passage  
7. possible sublunary settling  
8. Moon threshold  
9. possible Phantasia settling  
10. Moon test

## Critical distinction
Body dissolution and garment release are different.

### Body dissolution
- happens at death
- elemental
- concerns the body

### Garment release
- happens in ascent
- planetary
- concerns identification

## Sublunary zone
- ungoverned
- all seven garments still active
- first possible stopping region after death

Sublunary stopping conditions:
- Hylē
- Hēdonē
- Physis

These arise from elemental residue, not garment attachment.

## Manner-of-death modifiers

The cosmic structure of the journey is identical for all souls regardless of how death occurred. The soul's state — its attachments, its development, its orientation — determines the journey, not the death circumstances.

| Death Type | Initial Effect | Duration | Resolution |
|------------|----------------|----------|------------|
| **Peaceful/Prepared** | Minimal disorientation; smooth transition | Brief | Daimon visible quickly |
| **Expected but Difficult** (illness, age) | Some disorientation; processing needed | Moderate | Elemental Guardians provide comfort |
| **Sudden/Unexpected** | Significant disorientation; confusion about status | Extended | Daimon helps establish awareness of death |
| **Violent** | Potential psychic impression/trauma | Variable | Requires healing before full journey engagement |
| **Suicide** | Depends on soul's state (see below) | Variable | Treated with compassion, not punishment |

### Peaceful/prepared death
Often smoother transition. Less disorientation, quicker recognition of daimon, clearer understanding of what's happening. But still faces the same tests — preparation helps, it does not exempt.

### Sudden death
Often greater disorientation. Confusion about status. Extended orientation period. No penalty.

### Violent death
May leave strong trauma-imprint. This does NOT prevent the journey, create cosmic punishment, or trap the soul. It DOES require processing/healing, potentially extend the initial transition period, and sometimes create attachment to justice/revenge (which can cause Plateau-stuckness if unresolved). The Elemental Guardians and personal daimon help process violent death. The cosmos is healing, not punitive.

### Suicide
Compassion, not judgment. The soul's state at death matters, not the act itself:
- a soul who died in despair needs healing from the despair, not punishment for the death
- a soul who died in clarity (rare but possible) proceeds normally
- the journey addresses whatever led to the suicide — the underlying attachment or suffering
- there is NO special punishment, purgatory, or barrier for suicide

### Mass death
No queue. Structure unchanged. Population patterns surge.

### Severe mental illness / cognitive impairment
Brain is body-stuff. Soul essence is not distorted by bodily impairment, though lived pattern still matters.

### Children
Souls who die in childhood have not developed the full attachments that create Plateau-stuckness. They typically: transition smoothly (less to release), may pass through spheres quickly (less accumulated identification), may choose reincarnation to complete learning requiring embodied life. They are received with special gentleness by daimons and guardians. The cosmos adapts to each soul's development level.

## Compact Retrieval Block
- death changes transition, not destiny
- Guardians first

- sublunary zone is ungoverned
- Hylē, Hēdonē, Physis are residue-based
