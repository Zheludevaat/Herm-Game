# METAXY: The Soul's Ascent
## Full Game Design Brief

Prepared as a build-ready framework for a premium single-player narrative adventure RPG based on the Hermetic canon in this repository.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Game concept:** A premium top-down web-based Zelda-style adventure RPG in which the player guides a newly dead soul through the Metaxy, the seven planetary spheres, and a chain of seductive Plateaus that embody half-truths. The game is built on the internal Hermetic metaphysics of this repository: the cosmos is a school, not a prison; souls descend through the spheres to acquire garments needed for embodied life; after death, ascent requires recognizing and releasing those garments; daimons guide but do not solve; and Plateaus are voluntary stopping places where a soul mistakes a partial truth for the whole.

**Core promise:** The player does not level up by collecting stronger gear or larger numbers. The player advances by seeing more clearly, surrendering false identifications, and choosing continuation over comfort.

**Genre blend:** Narrative adventure, environmental exploration, inquiry-driven dialogue RPG, symbolic action, puzzle-trial boss encounters, light systemic immersion.

**One-line pitch:**
A soul dies, meets the being that has always walked beside it, and must pass through beauty, reason, heroism, struggle, mercy, and death itself without mistaking any of them for the destination.

## 2. Canon and Non-Negotiable World Rules

The game treats this repository's framework as canon. The following rules are mandatory.

### 2.1 Cosmology
- The metaphysical hierarchy is: **The One -> Nous -> World Soul -> Seven Spheres -> Sublunary embodied life**.
- The soul descends through the spheres and acquires garments that make embodied life possible.
- The ascent is not an escape from a bad creation. It is a graduation through understanding and release.
- The cosmos is alive, meaningful, and fundamentally educational.

### 2.2 Anti-Gnostic orientation
- The game must not treat matter as evil.
- The body must never be framed as a prison in the game's truth-state.
- A Gnostic reading can appear as a late-game error state or false doctrine within a Plateau, but the game's ontological truth is Hermetic, not Gnostic.

### 2.3 Daimon rules
- Every soul has a daimon.
- The daimon can guide during life through dreams, intuitions, questions, arranged encounters, and discomfort.
- After death, the daimon can accompany the soul in the Metaxy and to thresholds.
- The daimon cannot enter the interior of a Plateau.
- The daimon cannot enter the Governor's testing domain inside a sphere.
- The daimon waits at the boundary and reappears when the soul emerges.

### 2.4 Plateau rules
- Plateaus are not punishments. They are voluntary settlements created by resonance.
- Each Plateau contains a genuine insight mixed with a distortion.
- Souls stay because the Plateau flatters a wound or identity.
- A Plateau can only be exited by recognition and choice, not by force.
- Soft failure is choosing to remain. This is reversible from the meta menu after seeing consequence.

### 2.5 Sphere rules
- Each sphere is governed by a living intelligence, not a standard boss monster.
- Tests reveal what remains in the soul. They are examinations, not punitive trials.
- Each sphere asks for release of one garment.
- Governors:
  - Moon: **Selenos**
  - Mercury: **Hermaia**
  - Venus: **Kypria**
  - Sun: **Helion**
  - Mars: **Areon**
  - Jupiter: **Jovian**
  - Saturn: **Kronikos**

### 2.6 Reception after death
- The soul is received by Four Elemental Guardians:
  - **Zephyros**: clarity, orientation
  - **Pyralis**: purification, shock release
  - **Undine**: grief held and soothed
  - **Chthonia**: grounding, ontological stability
- They circle three times. This is fixed ritual language in-world.

### 2.7 Thematic law
The player must repeatedly feel that:
- partial truths are real but insufficient,
- identity can harden into a trap,
- release is harder than conquest,
- love and participation reveal more than contempt and escape,
- the path upward is movement into reality, not away from it.

## 4. High Concept Experience

The player begins in embodied life, in a short interactive prologue establishing:
1. what the soul loved,
2. what the soul feared,
3. what identity the soul mistook for itself.

Death occurs within ~20 minutes. The player is received by the Elemental Guardians, meets their daimon, enters the Metaxy, and chooses to move toward the Moon or drift into a resonant Plateau.

Core pattern:
1. Threshold arrival
2. Exploration and orientation
3. Plateau temptation or direct continuation
4. Investigation through dialogue, memory, symbolic encounters
5. Recognition of dominant garment
6. Sphere test
7. Release scene
8. Boundary reunion with daimon
9. Advance, return, or remain

Each major area includes:
- social hub,
- one central metaphysical distortion,
- 2 to 4 side quests,
- one major recognition scene,
- one threshold choice,
- one climactic test.

The player is never collecting the best sword or armor. The player is learning to carry less.

## 5. Game Pillars

1. **Walk the metaphysics** — setting learned through play, not lore dumps.
2. **Partial truth is the enemy, not evil** — leave by seeing limits, not denouncing reality.
3. **Progress is release** — highest beats are acts of relinquishment.
4. **The daimon is emotional spine** — companion of truth, not objective marker.
5. **Challenge without cynicism** — difficulty as learning, not cruelty.
6. **Symbolic gameplay, not abstract essay** — concrete verbs and choices.
7. **Humor remains alive** — warm irony around fixation and spiritual bureaucracy.

## 6. Player Character Framework

### 6.1 Protagonist model
- One soul with guided authored arc
- Player customizes mortal surface in prologue
- Working title: **The Pilgrim**
- Player-facing selections: mortal name, pronouns, one of three callings

### 6.2 Mortal callings
- **Scholar**
  - Strength: inquiry
  - Shadow: abstraction, over-explanation
- **Caregiver**
  - Strength: compassion
  - Shadow: savior identity, usefulness addiction
- **Reformer**
  - Strength: will and justice
  - Shadow: heroism, control, righteousness

### 6.3 Core wound
All openings converge: the soul mistook a garment for essence and sought worth through that mistake.

### 6.4 Why this structure works
- Keeps one coherent ascent arc
- Makes Plateau resonance personal
- Avoids blank-slate detachment
- Keeps production scope manageable

### 6.5 The daimon
Working name: **Sophene**

Presentation:
- appears ordinary at first glance,
- impossible signature in timing/shadow/light,
- patient, warm, exact speech,
- never forces choice,
- knows soul intimately but not future choices.

Functions:
- emotional truth anchor,
- boundary companion,
- memory prompt,
- mirror of what soul could become without distortion.

## 7. Core Story Arc

- **Act III: Venus** (love vs possession)
- **Act IV: Sun** (self without audience)
- **Act V: Mars** (strength in service)
- **Act VI: Jupiter** (systems for souls, not self)

- **Epilogue: Beyond the Spheres**

## 8. Macro Structure and Content Scope

**Shipping structure**
- 1 mortal prologue
- 1 reception/tutorial space
- 1 Metaxy connective world
- 7 sphere test spaces
- 7 paired Plateau acts
- 28–35 side quests
- 21 major memory scenes
- 7 Sophene boundary reunions
- 4 full endings + soft Plateau endings

**Runtime goals**
- Mainline: 28–35 hours
- Completionist: 40–50 hours

**Production tiers**
- Tier A (ship): 7 acts with 1 major Plateau + 1 minor district each
- Tier B (expansion): additional incarnations, optional full Plateaus, return-by-vow mode

3. Venus — **Eternal Biennale** + Ladder of Lovers
4. Sun — **Hall of Illuminated Testimony** + Sanctuary of Radiance

6. Jupiter — **Grand Tribunal of Perfect Justice** + Messianic Camp
7. Saturn — **Avenue of Accepted Fate** + Gnostic Threshold

Each pairing includes one exit insight centered on partial truth becoming identity.

## 10. Act-by-Act Design (Condensed)

- **Act I / Moon:** reflection, dream architecture, witness over image; Selenos puzzle-trial.
- **Act II / Mercury:** paradox paths, rhetorical evasion, naming true relation; Hermaia path/speech trial.
- **Act III / Venus:** beauty and attachment, attunement over possession; Kypria attraction/surrender trial.
- **Act IV / Sun:** exposure and persona, truth without audience; Helion truth-light trial.
- **Act V / Mars:** force, readiness, purpose, restraint; Areon will duel.
- **Act VI / Jupiter:** order, justice, mercy, consequence; Jovian systemic consequence trial.
- **Act VII / Saturn:** finitude, gratitude, surrender vs contempt; Kronikos stripping/silence trial.

## 11. Core Gameplay Loop

### 11.1 Moment-to-moment
Traverse -> notice dissonance -> dialogue/memory inquiry -> apply verbs -> resolve local quest -> gain Clarity/reduce Garment Weight -> confront cracking question -> release or rationalize -> Governor trial.

### 11.2 Plateau loop
Arrive in resonant comfort -> participate -> receive reward -> feel unresolved ache -> find crack -> act on crack -> walk to edge.

### 11.3 Sphere loop
Enter alone -> symbolic confrontation -> purified verb use -> answer via play/dialogue -> release garment -> reunite with Sophene.

## 12. Player Verbs and Mechanical Language

Base verbs: **Walk, Observe, Converse, Remember, Still**.

Sphere verbs:
- Moon: **Witness**
- Mercury: **Name**
- Venus: **Attune**
- Sun: **Expose**
- Mars: **Stand**
- Jupiter: **Weigh**
- Saturn: **Release**

Each verb must function in traversal, dialogue, quests, puzzles, and Governor trials.

## 13. Core Systems

- **Coherence** (health-equivalent presence resource; obscuration reset on depletion)
- **Clarity** (truth-growth currency; no random drops, no purchase)
- **Garment Weight** (7 garments with dynamic behavioral/gameplay influence)
- **Resonance Profile** (hidden matrix influencing invitations/NPC responses/endings)
- **Memory Lattice** (21 shards; at least one joyful memory per act)
- **Daimon Bond** (trust/honesty guidance relationship with Sophene)

## 14. Encounters, Failure, Difficulty

Encounter classes:
1. Dissonance encounters
2. Resonance duels
3. Governor trials

Difficulty modes:
- Story Mode
- Standard Mode
- Contemplative Challenge

Accessibility principle: challenge must not require opacity.

## 15. Dialogue System

Inquiry wheel actions:
- Observe
- Ask
- Name
- Challenge
- Confess
- Release
- Remain silent

Silence is mechanically valid and sometimes the only correct response.

## 16. Quest Design Framework

Quest categories:
- Cracking Question (mandatory)
- Soul Case (optional substantial mirrors)
- Memory Quests
- Metaxy Interludes

Rule: every quest must reveal garment, expose distortion, deepen relationship, or alter readiness for next question.

## 17. Progression and Economy

No loot treadmill, no cash economy clutter, no crafting bloat.

Progression comes through:
- Clarity,
- Memory shards,
- meaningful relics,
- perceptual upgrades,
- voluntary relinquishment.

## 20. Art Direction

Style: painterly metaphysical realism; each zone should make an ontological argument.

Domain-specific shape/color language preserved per sphere and Metaxy.

## 21. Audio Direction

Music: liturgical/intimate/psychologically precise.

Silence is active gameplay material, especially late Saturn.

## 22. Technical Design and Implementation

Recommended engine: **Unreal Engine 5**.

Narrative scripting: **Ink (or equivalent)** with variable/state tagging for garments, silence, localization, and testability.

Prototype first:
- traversal
- inquiry wheel dialogue
- Coherence/Clarity
- Witness + Name verbs
- one Plateau hub
- one Governor trial
- one Sophene boundary reunion

## 23. Production Plan

- Team: ~65–90 developers
- Pre-production: 8–10 months
- Production: 20–24 months
- Alpha: 4 months
- Beta/cert: 3–4 months
- Total: 35–42 months

Vertical slice target: full Act I flow from mortal prologue through Selenos trial and Sophene reunion.

## 24. Narrative Bible Rules

Tone: humane, exact, spiritually serious, often gently funny.

Avoid: nihilism, generic prophecy fantasy voice, pure bureaucratic satire, didactic exposition dumps, sinner-on-trial framing.

## 25. Risks and Mitigations

Core risks: abstraction overload, literary-over-gameplay imbalance, Plateau repetition, overwritten dialogue, Gnostic misread, didactic endings, scope creep.

Mitigation strategy: concrete verbs, symbolic systems, differentiated social textures, brevity/silence, systemic anti-Gnostic design, state-driven endings, paired Plateau scope controls.

## 26. Shipping Checklist

Includes critical path, side content, and polish gates preserving core canon features.

## 27. Final Creative Recommendation

Build this as a **release fantasy**, not a power fantasy.

Player fantasy:
- I am seen all the way through.
- I learn what I have carried.
- I stop mistaking garment for self.
- I continue.

## 28. Appendix A: Canon Extraction Summary

Immutable truths:
- The One -> Nous -> World Soul -> cosmos
- Sphere descent order: Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon
- Souls descend to learn/acquire garments
- Ascent requires release
- Cosmos is school, not prison
- Daimons guide and do not compel
- Plateaus are partial-truth settlements
- Governors test readiness, not worthiness for punishment
- Gratitude and participation open; contempt and escape-fixation close

## 29. Appendix B: Recommended Build Priorities

If cuts are necessary:
1. reduce attached district complexity,
2. reduce side-quest count,
3. reduce cinematic count,
4. reduce cosmetics,
5. reduce optional endings only last.

Do not cut:
- Sophene scenes
- Governor trials
- Elemental reception
- Memory lattice
- garment system
- Hermetic truth-state
