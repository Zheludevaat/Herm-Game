## Historical figures — Placement principles

1. Place by actual philosophy, not caricature. Epicurus goes to a Plateau that reflects actual Epicurean philosophy (moderate pleasure, friendship, absence of fear), not the popular caricature (hedonism).

2. Multiple possibilities. Many figures could be at several Plateaus depending on which aspect of their thought is emphasized. The Series Bible must make specific choices and stick to them.

3. Evolution allowed. Historical figures may have modified their views after death/time. They can have doubts about their own systems.

4. Respect required. All historical figures are portrayed with respect for their genuine contributions. They are not fools or villains — they are thinkers who found something real and stopped too soon.

5. Living figures prohibited. No living people as characters.
