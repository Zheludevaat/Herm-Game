# 30_VOICE_AND_STYLE_BINDING_RULES

Status: Binding  
Authority level: Tier 2  
Primary scope: Style law for all stories

## Absolute narrative convictions
- Hermetic philosophy is true inside the anthology
- comedy is the correct mode
- genuine learning requires experience
- intellectual understanding alone is insufficient
- the cosmos is wonderful

## Philosophy through experience
Never lecture a concept in first form.
Always dramatize it through character experience.

## Anti-preaching law
- Governors teach souls, not readers
- daimons counsel souls, not readers
- narration shows and implies
- cut explanation after demonstration

## Prohibition list
Never use:
- generic cosmic vagueness
- decorative spirituality language
- profundity voice
- adverb-modified said
- recap dialogue
- passive mystical fog
- long unbroken wise monologues
- solemnity without humor

## Register system
Available registers:
- comic-satiric
- mythic-lyrical
- philosophical-dramatic
- intimate-psychological
- observational-anthropological

Binding rule:
no more than two pages without humor of any kind

Exception: Ennead Interludes 3 (The Woman Who Needed No Story) and 7 (The Garment That Wore the Soul) are intentionally humorless — the only two pieces in the anthology that violate this rule. Their silence where comedy should be is deliberate: one shows the system working perfectly, the other shows its worst-case failure. Two exceptions at opposite poles prove the rule exists. See SCOTT_ALEXANDER_INTERLUDE_DESIGN_V2.md.

## Technical defaults
- ~9,000 words
- 3 chapters
- average scene 800 to 1,500 words
- audio readability wins when print and audio conflict
- maximum 6 footnotes per chapter

## Scene law
Situation -> complication -> turn

Binding sub-rule:
the funniest moment and the deepest insight should fuse at the turn.

## Compact Retrieval Block
- philosophy through experience
- comedy mandatory
- no lecture
- ~9,000 words
- 3 chapters
- audio wins
- scene turn fuses joke and insight
