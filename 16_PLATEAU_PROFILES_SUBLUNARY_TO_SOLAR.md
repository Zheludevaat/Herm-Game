# 16_PLATEAU_PROFILES_SUBLUNARY_TO_SOLAR

Status: Canonical  
Authority level: Tier 1  
Primary scope: Hylē through Eidōlon

## Global profile rule
Every Plateau profile must keep four things visible:
- the truth it genuinely holds
- the error that makes it a stopping place
- the noble face
- the degraded face

## Hylē
Attachment: substance  
Truth: matter is real  
Error: matter is all  
Crack usually comes when reality proves more concrete than physicalism can explain

## Hēdonē
Attachment: sensation  
Truth: pleasure is real  
Error: pleasure is the ceiling  
Crack often comes through repetition, exhaustion, or pleasure opening beyond itself

## Physis
Attachment: natural process  
Truth: nature is sacred  
Error: nature suffices as ultimate explanation  
Crack often comes when beauty or being exceeds closed naturalism

## Phantasia
Attachment: imagination  
Truth: imagination reveals  
Error: image is reality / becoming replaces arrival  
Faces: static dreamer, endless becomer

## Metis
Attachment: cleverness  
Truth: reason illuminates  
Error: map is territory  
Faces: system-builder, skeptic, classifier, elegant self-underminer

## Eros
Attachment: desire  
Truth: beauty participates in Beauty  
Error: particular is universal / grasping is love  
Never write Eros as anti-love

## Eidōlon
Attachment: self-image  
Truth: identity and excellence are real  
Error: phantom is soul  
Faces: sincere hero, hollow performer  
Never write the sincere face as fake

## Compact Retrieval Block
- Hylē = matter is all
- Hēdonē = pleasure is highest
- Physis = nature suffices
- Phantasia = image is reality
- Metis = map is territory
- Eros = particular is universal
- Eidōlon = phantom is soul
