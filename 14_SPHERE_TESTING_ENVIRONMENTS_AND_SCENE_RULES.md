# 14_SPHERE_TESTING_ENVIRONMENTS_AND_SCENE_RULES

Status: Canonical / Operational  
Authority level: Tier 1 for facts, Tier 3 for scene-use  
Primary scope: Sphere testing environments

## Absolute distinction
Testing environment is inside the sphere. Plateau is outside in the threshold.

## Environment law
Each sphere has:
- invariant quality
- variable imagery translation

Quality is cosmological. Imagery is perceptual.

## Presence rules during tests
- Governor always present
- personal daimon waits outside
- other souls are not present
- sphere-daimons may coalesce as aspects of the threshold ecology, not as extra test participants

## Sphere-by-Sphere Environments

### MOON (Selenos)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Light | Silver, shifting, reflective — like moonlight on water |
| Space | Dreamlike, boundaries unclear, things shift when not directly observed |
| Surfaces | Mirrors, still water, reflective surfaces everywhere |
| Sound | Echoes, whispers, the faint music of distant spheres |
| Temperature | Cool, not cold — the coolness of night |
| Sense | Liminality — standing at a threshold, between states |

Dominant sensory channel: unstable vision, silver liminality

### MERCURY (Hermaia)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Light | Quicksilver, iridescent, changing color and intensity |
| Space | Boundary-space — neither here nor there, doors everywhere |
| Surfaces | Shifting — walls become passages, passages become walls |
| Sound | Many voices, paradoxes echoing, riddles in the air |
| Temperature | Variable — changes without pattern |
| Sense | Trickster energy — nothing quite what it seems, but not malicious |

Dominant sensory channel: sound, overlap, paradox, quicksilver boundary-space

### VENUS (Kypria)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Light | Rose-gold, warm, intimate — light that flatters |
| Space | Garden, bower, intimate chamber — beauty manifest |
| Surfaces | Soft, curved, inviting — nothing harsh |
| Sound | Music, laughter, the beloved's voice |
| Temperature | Warm, perfect — the temperature of embrace |
| Sense | Desire — everything beautiful, everything calling |

Dominant sensory channel: touch and smell, intimate beauty, longing

### SUN (Helion)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Light | Blinding gold, then somehow visible — no shadows ANYWHERE |
| Space | Open, exposed, nowhere to hide — noon in the desert |
| Surfaces | Transparent — everything seen, nothing hidden |
| Sound | Silence, then truth — the voice that cannot be ignored |
| Temperature | Hot but not burning — the heat of revelation |
| Sense | Exposure — everything you are is visible; no pretense survives |

Dominant sensory channel: exposure, blinding clarity, no hiding

### MARS (Areon)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Light | Red-tinged, focused, martial — the light of fire and forge |
| Space | Arena, training ground, battlefield — a space of testing |
| Surfaces | Hard, unyielding, demanding — iron and stone |
| Sound | Drums, commands, the clash of will against obstacle |
| Temperature | Hot with exertion — the heat of effort |
| Sense | Challenge — everything demands response; passivity impossible |

Dominant sensory channel: kinesthetic pressure, exertion, challenge

### JUPITER (Jovian)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Light | Royal blue deepening to purple — majestic, weighty |
| Space | Throne room, court, judgment hall — formal and consequential |
| Surfaces | Rich, ordered, proper — everything in its right place |
| Sound | Resonant, authoritative — voices that carry weight |
| Temperature | Temperate, balanced — neither too hot nor too cold |
| Sense | Consequence — everything matters; everything is weighed |

Dominant sensory channel: scale, consequence, ordered weight

### SATURN (Kronikos)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Light | Dim, leaden, ancient — the light of old stars |
| Space | Edge-space, threshold, the place where things end — vast but bounded |
| Surfaces | Stone, lead, the weight of ages — nothing moves quickly |
| Sound | Slow, deep, patient — silences that last epochs |
| Temperature | Cold but not painful — the cold of deep time |
| Sense | Finitude — everything ends; the boundary of everything |

Dominant sensory channel: time as sensation, finitude, patient bounded vastness

## Scene rules
- identify the sphere through sensory logic before naming it
- environment begins the challenge before the Governor sharpens it
- avoid generic mystical rooms
- if the scene could be moved to another sphere with minor edits, it is too generic

## Compact Retrieval Block
- sphere test inside sphere
- Plateau outside in threshold
- quality fixed, imagery variable
- Governor always present
- each sphere has distinct sensory grammar:
  - Moon: silver shifting light, mirrors, echoes, cool, liminality
  - Mercury: quicksilver iridescent, shifting walls/passages, paradox voices, trickster energy
  - Venus: rose-gold warm light, garden/bower, soft surfaces, music, desire
  - Sun: blinding gold, no shadows, transparent surfaces, silence then truth, exposure
  - Mars: red-tinged focused light, arena/battlefield, iron and stone, drums, challenge
  - Jupiter: royal blue to purple, throne room, rich ordered surfaces, resonant voices, consequence
  - Saturn: dim leaden ancient light, edge-space, stone and lead, slow deep silences, finitude
