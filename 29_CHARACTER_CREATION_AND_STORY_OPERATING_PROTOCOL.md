# 29_CHARACTER_CREATION_AND_STORY_OPERATING_PROTOCOL

Status: Binding  
Authority level: Tier 3  
Primary scope: Story planning workflow

## Required planning sequence
1. choose soul essence
2. determine incarnation history
3. determine Plateau or sphere susceptibility
4. design personal daimon
5. determine manner of death if relevant
6. determine garment configuration
7. determine arc ending

## Story packet
Before drafting, create a compact packet with:
- soul essence
- incarnation history
- current level / Plateau / sphere
- manner of death
- garment configuration
- daimon profile
- primary philosophical tension
- story archetype
- ending state

## Story engine sentence
Every story needs one sentence in the form:
A soul who wants X is trapped by Y and can only crack when Z becomes unavoidable.

## Constraints
- do not choose Plateau first and reverse-engineer lazily
- do not leave daimon generic
- do not confuse cracking with transformation
- do not choose ending without earned setup

## Compact Retrieval Block
- essence first
- history second
- susceptibility third
- daimon fourth
- death fifth
- configuration sixth
- arc seventh
- one-screen story packet required
