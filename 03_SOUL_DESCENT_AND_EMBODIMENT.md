# 03_SOUL_DESCENT_AND_EMBODIMENT

Status: Canonical  
Authority level: Tier 1  
Primary scope: Descent, birth, lethe, first incarnation versus return

## Descent sequence

1. Departure from World Soul
2. Passage through Fixed Stars
3. Acquisition of seven garments from Saturn through Moon
4. Entry into sublunary existence
5. Birth at first breath

Hard canon:
- descent is obscuration, not conscious testing
- soul does not consciously negotiate spheres during descent
- soul enters full embodied life only after all garments are in place

## Why souls incarnate
- to learn what only embodiment teaches
- to express divine reality through particularity
- to exercise capacities under time, vulnerability, sensation, and limitation

## Lethe

### What it erases
- conscious memory of origin
- conscious memory of descent
- explicit past-life recall
- conscious awareness of daimon

### What it does not erase
- essence
- pattern
- daimonic bond
- nous
- affinities
- recurring tendencies

Hard rule:
Lethe removes explicit memory, not ontological continuity.

## First-timers and returners

### First-timer
- eternal essence
- no accumulated pattern
- daimon assigned for the journey
- first life configured by essence + cosmic moment

### Returning soul
- same essence
- accumulated pattern
- same daimon
- life configured by essence + pattern + cosmic moment

Critical equality:
- same structure
- same Governors
- same Plateaus
- same opportunity for completion

## Manner of pre-birth choice
Preferred formulation: the soul *leans* toward incarnation.
Not catalog-shopping. Not bureaucratic assignment.

## Minimum daimon setup
- present before birth
- accompanies descent
- remembers what the soul forgets
- cannot choose for the soul
- bond becomes hidden at birth

## Compact Retrieval Block
- descent is obscuration, not testing
- incarnation is opportunity
- lethe removes conscious memory, not continuity
- first-timer = essence + cosmic moment
- returner = essence + pattern + cosmic moment
- same daimon across all incarnations
- soul leans toward incarnation
