## Personal daimons — Registry

Personal daimons are created during story planning and tracked here for consistency. Each soul has one personal daimon, the same across all incarnations. Daimon names must remain consistent if a soul recurs.

### Design principles for personal daimons
- Each daimon expresses care through a distinctive quality (e.g., precision, silence, humor, patience, fierceness)
- The daimon's personality should complement or contrast the soul's essence
- The daimon has been with the soul since individuation
- Daimon names should feel classical/mythic without being directly borrowed from existing mythology
- Daimons have complete memory of all the soul's past lives across all incarnations
- A daimon may decline to share its name in contexts where the name would not yet serve

## Fast anti-drift rules

- Psychopompos is a sphere-daimon, not a personal daimon
- Pneumara is not separate from World Soul
- Mnema is the World Soul threshold-keeper, not Pneumara — they are distinct roles
- Nous-Keeper has no ordinary appearance, no face, no personality (but may manifest for specific soul's mode)
- Sphere-daimons are not staff and do not hold friendly conversation beside Governors
- Governor courts have named members (see rosters above); they appear at Governor's discretion, not automatically
- Court members may encounter souls independently for specialized purpose; these encounters are gentler than Governor tests
- Plateau architecture is generated by stuck souls, not by cosmic beings
- Governor is inside sphere proper, not administering the plateau
- Personal daimon cannot enter a plateau
- Personal daimon waits outside Governor tests
- Personal daimon waits at World Soul outer edge — does not pass through with the soul
- One personal daimon per soul, same across all incarnations
- Daimon names must remain consistent when a soul recurs
- Jupiter Governor canonical name is Jovian — not Dios
- Speaking Guardians: exceptional capacity, canonical at death threshold (Story 27 onward)
