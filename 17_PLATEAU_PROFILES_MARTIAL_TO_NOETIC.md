# 17_PLATEAU_PROFILES_MARTIAL_TO_NOETIC

Status: Canonical  
Authority level: Tier 1  
Primary scope: Thumos, Philotimia, Anankē, Gnosis

## Higher-Plateau rule
The higher the Plateau, the more accurate the truth holding the soul.
These souls are not stupider. They are often more lucid and more poignant.

## Thumos
Attachment: will  
Truth: courage and struggle are real  
Error: battle is purpose  
Crack often comes when laying down the sword does not destroy the self

## Philotimia
Attachment: ambition / significance / order-hunger  
Truth: justice and significance are real  
Error: system is reality  
Crack often comes when perfect ordering fails to account for itself

## Anankē
Attachment: structure  
Truth: limit and finitude are real  
Error: boundary is final  
Faces: structuralist, fatalist  
Crack often comes from grace or reality that does not fit the framework

## Gnosis
Attachment: knowledge itself  
Truth: the soul really knows much of the cosmic structure  
Error: knowing is being  
This is not a sphere Plateau and not “Mercury but smarter”

## Compact Retrieval Block
- Thumos = battle is purpose
- Philotimia = system is reality
- Anankē = boundary is final
- Gnosis = knowing is being
