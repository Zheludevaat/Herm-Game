## What Plateaus are
Plateaus are:
- real
- comfortable
- partial
- voluntary
- escapable

Souls stay because they found something genuinely good and stopped there.

Plateaus are not:
- punishments
- prisons
- ideology bins
- illusions only

## Population Dynamics

Plateaus are not static. Their populations fluctuate based on historical conditions in the living world, dominant ideologies of each era, which errors are culturally reinforced, reincarnation outflows, and release events.

**Population Gradient:**
Lower Plateaus catch more souls (first stopping points). Each level thins population (only those who passed lower tests can get stuck higher). Nous-level stuckness is rare.

**Population Distribution (Current Era):**

| Stopping Condition | Population Level | Trend | Notes |
|--------------------|------------------|-------|-------|
| Hylē (Material) | VERY HIGH | Rising sharply (post-1800) | Scientific materialism; largest modern influx |
| Hēdonē (Appetitive) | HIGH | Rising | Consumer culture; hedonic optimization |
| Physis (Natural) | MODERATE | Stable | Always present; alpha ideology |
| Phantasia (Imaginative/Growth faces) | HIGH | Rising sharply | Media saturation; self-improvement industry |
| Metis (Rationalist/Skeptical faces) | MODERATE-HIGH | Stable-Rising | Intellectual class; academic overflow |
| Eros (Aesthetic/Romantic faces) | LOW-MODERATE | Stable | Artist subculture; romantic idealization |
| Eidōlon (Heroic/Performative faces) | HIGH | Rising sharply | Social media; spiritual influencers; achievement culture |
| Thumos (Warrior/Revolutionary faces) | LOW-MODERATE | Cyclical | Fewer martial cultures; surges with political upheavals |
| Philotimia (Legalist/Messianic faces) | MODERATE | Stable | Legal/justice professions; helper professions |
| Anankē (Structuralist/Fatalist faces) | LOW-MODERATE | Stable-Rising | Systems thinkers; determinism's return |
| Gnosis (Gnostic/Intellectual faces) | LOW to VERY LOW | Cyclical | Esoteric revivals; few reach Nous |

**Historical Clustering by Era:**

| Era | Dominant Stopping Conditions |
|-----|------------------------------|
| Prehistoric | Physis, Thumos |
| Classical | Metis, Eidōlon, Eros |
| Medieval | Eidōlon (performative face), Philotimia (messianic face), Thumos |
| Early Modern | Metis, Thumos (revolutionary face) |
| Contemporary | Hylē, Phantasia (growth face), Eidōlon (performative face), Hēdonē |

**Release Rates:**
- Self-reinforcing conditions (Hylē, Metis, Eros-aesthetic, Anankē) release slowly
- Self-exhausting conditions (Hēdonē, Phantasia-growth, Eidōlon-performative, Philotimia-messianic) release at moderate rates
- Gnosis releases extremely slowly — genuine satisfaction makes stuckness invisible

**Outflow Destinations:**
- ~30% continue ascending (attempt next sphere test)
- ~60% reincarnate (need embodied experience to develop capacity)
- ~10% settle at another stopping condition

## External Appearance (Psychopompos's view)

From outside, Plateaus appear as distinct regions in the cosmic geography — visible concentrations of soul-light, bounded but permeable (no walls, but clear edges), organized by level.

| Level | External Appearance |
|-------|---------------------|
| Sublunary | Dense, earth-toned, heavy; like sediment at bottom of clear water |
| Moon | Silver-shifting, dreamlike edges; hard to focus on directly |
| Mercury | Crystalline, geometric, sharp angles; patterns that almost make sense |
| Venus | Rose-gold luminosity; beautiful from outside; music almost audible |
| Sun | Bright, stage-lit, shadows and spotlights; performance visible |
| Mars | Red-tinged, active, conflict-energy visible as motion |
| Jupiter | Purple-blue, ordered, structures visible; weight of consequence |
| Saturn | Leaden, ancient, slow; time moves visibly differently |
| Nous-threshold | Bright but bounded; so close to Nous it almost glows |

**Observable from outside:** population density, movement patterns, edge activity (daimons waiting at boundaries), crack events (visible as brightening), social structures (leadership, clusters, factions visible as patterns), historical layers (older souls vs. newer arrivals distinguishable).
