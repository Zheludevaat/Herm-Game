## Ennead Interludes
Between certain stories, 9 Ennead Interludes provide meta-commentary, hidden information, and a sequential returner (Epistrophos) thread. Labeled with Proclean operations (Monē/Proodos/Epistrophē) across three levels (Being/Life/Intellect). See SCOTT_ALEXANDER_INTERLUDE_DESIGN_V2.md for full specifications and placements.

36 stories + 9 Ennead interludes = 45 total sections (the triangular number of the Ennead).

Density: Movement I has 1 interlude, Movement II has 6, Movement III has 2 (final 6 stories uninterrupted). No two interludes appear consecutively without a story between them.

## Recurring architecture
- 9 Ennead Interludes placed between stories (see SCOTT_ALEXANDER_INTERLUDE_DESIGN_V2.md for exact placements)
- Psychopompos content appears in Ennead 3 (closing observation), Ennead 8 (private field notes — entire interlude)
- At least one soul should recur across multiple stories (recurring soul arc spanning the anthology)
- Recurring daimons may recur if tracked consistently
- An Epistrophos figure (the Return; sole named mythic soul) threads through the Ennead interludes (margin note → statistical anomaly → watching presence → philosophical question → direct speech → full reveal → failure → sighting → deepest question) and appears in Story 31
- Named plateau residents (real stuck souls, not architecture) populate Movement II stories; some appear in codas showing small changes after the protagonist's crisis
- Plateau codas in selected Movement II stories — brief epilogues showing the plateau world slightly altered. These are NOT separate stories; they are closing beats within the story they belong to.
- Final story concludes with a braided coda tracking characters and locations across the full anthology

## Reader progression
The reader's journey mirrors the soul's journey, but this must never be stated directly in the fiction.
